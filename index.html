Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Can I change my ticket plan later?

Yes! You can upgrade your ticket from Standard to Pro at any time before October 1st, 2026, by paying the difference via your user profile panel.

Are team tickets shareable between colleagues?

Team passes are assigned to specific individuals for security and badge printing. However, you can transfer a name registration up to 14 days before the event starts.

Will sessions be recorded for remote viewing?

Absolutely! All keynotes and main track presentations are recorded. Standard pass holders get 30 days of access, while Pro and Team tiers get unlimited lifetime access.
